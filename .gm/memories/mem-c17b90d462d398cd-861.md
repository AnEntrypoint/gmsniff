gm watcher.log parsing: only ~20% of lines are "evt: {json}" records (measured 26836 of 132387 across 3 live projects). The discarded 80% carries the highest-value live-agent signal and MUST be parsed as structured non-JSON lines: "[dispatch] -> verb=<v> task=<n> body=<n>b" is the dispatch-START stream (there is no dispatch.start evt record -- this line is the only source, and it pairs with dispatch.end {ms,verb} for in-flight verb tracking with duration); "[plugkit-wasm] plugkit vX.Y.Z (wasm)" is the real per-project served version now that .status.json dropped its version field; "--- daemon spawn <ISO> parent=<pid> ---" gives process lineage and per-boot epoch boundaries; "[plugkit-wasm] stale lock (holder pid=N dead, age=Nms); taking over" is a real contention/recovery signal; "[retention] swept N out/ files older than 1h" quantifies spool churn.
